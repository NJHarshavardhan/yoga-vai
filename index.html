01 — Yoga, for the young

More than exercise. A first practice of stillness.

Yoga is a journey through movement, breath, strength, flexibility, balance and mindfulness — and for children, it's often the gentlest introduction to all five. Mindful Movement built the Joyful Yoga programme to give kids a real, embodied sense of calm before the day asks too much of them.
